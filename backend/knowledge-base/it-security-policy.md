# IT Security Policy

## Account Setup
All new employees will receive their corporate email address and system credentials within the first day of joining. Your IT point of contact for onboarding is the Service Desk team at it-support@company.com or extension 1234.

### Email Setup
Your corporate email format is firstname.lastname@company.com. Email is provisioned automatically by IT. You will receive a temporary password via your personal email. Change your password immediately upon first login. Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) within the first 24 hours.

### Slack Setup
- Download Slack from https://slack.com/download
- Sign in with your corporate email
- You will be auto-added to #general, #announcements, and your team channel
- Set your profile picture and display name

### VPN Setup
1. Download the VPN client from the IT portal at https://it-portal.company.com/vpn
2. Install the client on your machine
3. Log in with your corporate credentials
4. Select the nearest server location
5. VPN is required when accessing company resources from outside the office network

## Password Policy
- Minimum 12 characters
- Must include uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special characters
- Password must be changed every 90 days
- Cannot reuse the last 5 passwords
- Account locks after 5 failed login attempts

## Equipment Policy

### Requesting Equipment
Submit an equipment request through the IT Service Desk portal at https://it-portal.company.com/request. Standard equipment includes a laptop, monitor, keyboard, mouse, and headset. Requests are typically fulfilled within 3-5 business days.

### Approved Software
Only software approved by the IT department may be installed on company devices. The approved software list is available on the IT portal. To request new software, submit a request through the Service Desk.

### Equipment Return
All company equipment must be returned upon separation from the company. Equipment must be returned to the IT department on or before the last working day.

## Security Training
All employees must complete the IT Security Awareness Training within the first 2 weeks of joining. The training covers:
- Phishing awareness and how to identify suspicious emails
- Safe browsing practices
- Data handling and classification
- Incident reporting procedures
- Physical security best practices

The training is available at https://learning.company.com/it-security. Annual refresher training is mandatory for all employees.

## Incident Reporting
If you suspect a security incident (phishing email, data breach, lost device, unauthorized access), report it immediately to:
- Email: security@company.com
- Phone: Extension 9999
- Slack: #security-incidents

Do not attempt to investigate security incidents on your own.

## Data Classification
- **Public**: Information intended for public distribution
- **Internal**: Information for internal use only (default classification)
- **Confidential**: Sensitive business information, customer data
- **Restricted**: Highly sensitive data, financial records, PII

Handle data according to its classification. Confidential and Restricted data must be encrypted in transit and at rest.
