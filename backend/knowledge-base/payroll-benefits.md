# Payroll & Benefits Guide

## Payroll Schedule
Payroll is processed on the 25th of every month. If the 25th falls on a weekend or holiday, payroll is processed on the preceding business day. Salary is credited to the bank account on file by the last working day of the month.

## Salary Components
Your total compensation includes the following components:
- **Basic Salary**: 40% of CTC (Cost to Company)
- **House Rent Allowance (HRA)**: 20% of CTC
- **Special Allowance**: Variable based on grade
- **Performance Bonus**: Up to 15% of annual salary, paid quarterly
- **Provident Fund**: 12% of basic salary (employer contribution)

## Tax Forms and Documentation

### W-4 / Tax Declaration
- All employees must submit their tax declaration within the first 30 days of joining
- Update your tax declaration at the beginning of each financial year (April)
- Submit proof of investments by January 31st each year
- Access the tax declaration form at https://hrms.company.com/tax

### Form 16
Form 16 is generated annually and available for download from the HRMS portal by June 15th of each year. This form is required for filing your income tax return.

## Setting Up Payroll
1. Log in to the HRMS portal
2. Navigate to "My Profile" → "Bank Details"
3. Enter your bank account number, IFSC code, and account holder name
4. Upload a cancelled cheque or bank statement for verification
5. Your payroll will be processed within 2 pay cycles after verification

## Benefits

### Health Insurance
All full-time employees are covered under the group health insurance plan from Day 1. Coverage includes:
- Employee: Up to ₹5,00,000 / $10,000 per year
- Spouse: Up to ₹3,00,000 / $6,000 per year
- Children (up to 2): Up to ₹2,00,000 / $4,000 per child per year
- Parents: Optional add-on available at subsidized rates

Claims can be filed through the insurance portal or by contacting benefits@company.com.

### Retirement Benefits
The company contributes 12% of your basic salary to the Employee Provident Fund (EPF). You can access your EPF account details at https://hrms.company.com/epf.

### Employee Stock Options (ESOP)
After completing 1 year of service, eligible employees may be granted stock options. Vesting period is 4 years with a 1-year cliff. Details are provided in your offer letter.

### Learning & Development
The company provides an annual learning budget of $1,500 per employee. This can be used for:
- Online courses and certifications
- Conference attendance
- Books and learning materials
- Professional memberships

Submit a learning expense request through the HRMS portal for approval.

### Meal Allowance
Employees working from the office receive a meal allowance of $10 per working day. The allowance is credited monthly along with salary.

### Transportation
Employees working late (after 8 PM) can avail company-provided cab service. Book through the transport portal or contact transport@company.com.

## Expense Reimbursement
Business expenses must be submitted within 30 days of incurrence. Submit receipts through the HRMS portal under "Expenses." Approval workflow: Manager → Finance → Payment within 15 business days.
