# Leave Policy

## Types of Leave

### Paid Time Off (PTO)
All full-time employees receive 20 days of PTO per year. PTO accrues at a rate of 1.67 days per month. Unused PTO can be carried over to the next year, up to a maximum of 10 days.

### Sick Leave
Employees receive 12 days of sick leave per year. Sick leave can be used for personal illness, medical appointments, or to care for an immediate family member who is ill. A medical certificate is required for sick leave exceeding 3 consecutive days.

### Casual Leave
Employees receive 7 days of casual leave per year for personal matters. Casual leave cannot be taken for more than 3 consecutive days without prior approval from the reporting manager.

### Maternity Leave
Female employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave for the first two children. Maternity leave can begin up to 8 weeks before the expected delivery date.

### Paternity Leave
Male employees are entitled to 2 weeks of paid paternity leave within 6 months of the birth or adoption of a child.

### Bereavement Leave
Employees are entitled to 5 days of paid bereavement leave upon the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, or sibling).

### Compensatory Off
Employees who work on public holidays or weekends with manager approval are entitled to compensatory time off within 30 days.

## How to Apply for Leave

1. Log in to the HRMS portal at https://hrms.company.com
2. Navigate to Leave Management from the dashboard
3. Click "Apply for Leave"
4. Select the leave type from the dropdown
5. Enter the start and end dates
6. Add a reason for the leave (optional for PTO, required for sick leave over 3 days)
7. Click "Submit Request"
8. Your reporting manager will be notified and must approve the request

## Leave During Probation
Employees in their probation period (first 6 months) are entitled to:
- 10 days PTO (prorated)
- 6 days Sick Leave (prorated)
- 3 days Casual Leave
Probation employees must have manager approval for any leave exceeding 2 consecutive days.

## Public Holidays
The company observes 10 public holidays per year. The holiday calendar is published at the beginning of each year on the HRMS portal. Additional regional holidays may be granted based on office location.

## Leave Approval Process
Leave requests must be submitted at least 3 business days in advance for planned leave. The reporting manager has 48 hours to approve or reject the request. If no action is taken within 48 hours, the request is auto-approved. Emergency leave requests can be submitted on the same day with a follow-up explanation within 24 hours.
