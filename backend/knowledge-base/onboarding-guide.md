# Onboarding Checklist Guide

## Welcome to Onboarding
This guide walks you through the complete onboarding process at our company. Following these steps ensures you have everything set up for a productive start.

## Day 1 — Getting Started

### Complete Your Profile
- Log in to the HRMS portal with your credentials
- Fill in your personal details: full name, phone number, emergency contacts
- Upload a professional profile photo
- Set your preferred communication channels

### Collect Your Equipment
- Visit the IT desk on Floor 2 to collect your laptop and accessories
- Sign the equipment acknowledgment form
- Set up your laptop with the provided instructions

### Meet Your Buddy
Every new joiner is assigned a buddy from their team. Your buddy will:
- Give you a tour of the office
- Introduce you to team members
- Help you navigate internal tools and processes
- Be available for questions during your first month

## Day 2 — Orientation

### Read the Company Handbook
Review the company handbook to understand our values, code of conduct, and workplace policies. The handbook is available at https://docs.company.com/handbook.

### Complete IT Security Training
Complete the mandatory IT security awareness training at https://learning.company.com/it-security. This covers phishing, password policies, and data handling.

### Set Up Your Tools
- Configure your email client
- Set up Slack and join required channels
- Install VPN client for remote access
- Set up the project management tool (Jira/Asana)

## Day 3 — Administrative Tasks

### Set Up Payroll
- Navigate to HRMS portal → My Profile → Bank Details
- Enter your bank information for salary deposits
- Submit your tax declaration

### Submit Required Documents
Upload the following documents to the HRMS portal:
1. Government-issued photo ID (passport, driver's license, or national ID)
2. Address proof (utility bill, bank statement, or lease agreement)
3. Tax form (W-4 or equivalent)
4. Educational certificates (degree certificates and transcripts)
5. Previous employment letter (if applicable)
6. Medical fitness certificate

### Review and Sign Policies
- Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)
- Employee Handbook Acknowledgment
- IT Acceptable Use Policy
- Code of Conduct Agreement

## Week 1 — Team Integration

### 1-on-1 with Manager
Schedule a 30-minute meeting with your reporting manager to discuss:
- Team structure and your role
- Short-term goals for your first 30 days
- Communication preferences
- Regular meeting schedule

### Team Introduction
Attend the team standup and introduce yourself. Share your background and what excites you about the role.

### Project Onboarding
Your manager or tech lead will assign you initial tasks to get familiar with the codebase, product, or project you'll be working on.

## Month 1 — Building Momentum

### Complete All Mandatory Training
Ensure you have completed:
- IT Security Training
- Company Values Workshop
- Compliance Training
- Role-specific technical training

### Monthly Check-in with HR
HR will schedule a check-in call to discuss your onboarding experience, address any concerns, and gather feedback.

### Set Your 90-Day Goals
Work with your manager to define clear, measurable goals for your first 90 days. These will be reviewed during your probation assessment.

## Probation Period
The standard probation period is 6 months. During probation:
- Monthly performance check-ins with your manager
- Mid-probation review at 3 months
- Final probation review at 6 months
- Successful completion leads to confirmation and full benefits
- HR will notify you of the outcome by email
